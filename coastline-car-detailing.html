Detailing Built for Salt Air & Beach Life

Living near the water is worth it — but salt air, sand, and sun take a real toll on a vehicle. Salt spray speeds up oxidation and water spotting, sand works its way into every seat crease and floor mat, and UV exposure fades trim and dashboards faster than inland neighborhoods ever see.

We regularly work the whole coastline, from Pacific Beach and Mission Beach down by the boardwalk, up through Ocean Beach, Del Mar, Solana Beach, and Encinitas, to Carlsbad and Oceanside. Street parking, beach lots, condos without a hose — we bring everything we need and set up wherever your car actually is.

  • ✔ Deep sand & salt residue removal, inside and out
  • ✔ Ceramic coating to fight salt air & UV fade
  • ✔ No hose, driveway, or outlet required

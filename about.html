About Golden Peanut's Detail

Bringing the shine back to San Diego, one vehicle at a time.

Golden Peanut's Detail is a San Diego based mobile detailing company with over 10 years of experience bringing the shine back to cars, trucks, boats, RVs, and more. We come to you — home, office, work, anywhere.

We're proud to support our local community and speak both English and Spanish. ¡Hablamos español!

Golden Peanut's Detail is co-founded and run by Daniel and Luna, who personally handle every detail — literally. When you book with us, they're the ones showing up to do the work.
